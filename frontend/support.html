Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and check your inbox for a reset link. Follow the instructions to create a new password. The link expires after 24 hours for security.

Is my personal information secure?

Yes, absolutely. ClinixPro uses enterprise-grade encryption (SSL/TLS 1.2), HIPAA-compliant security measures, and regular security audits. Your data is never shared with third parties without explicit consent.

Can I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Yes! You can cancel or reschedule appointments up to 24 hours before the scheduled time. Go to "My Appointments" and click the "Reschedule" or "Cancel" button. Check your plan details for any applicable cancellation fees.

How do I contact my doctor directly?

Use the "Messages" section in your dashboard to send messages to your doctor. Healthcare providers typically respond within 24 hours during business days. For urgent medical emergencies, use the emergency hotline.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), bank transfers, mobile wallets (JazzCash, Easypaisa), and installment plans through our payment partners.

How do I download my prescription?

Navigate to "Prescriptions" in your dashboard, find the prescription you need, and click the download icon. You can also print directly or share with a pharmacy electronically.

Is there a mobile app available?

ClinixPro works flawlessly on mobile browsers with full functionality. Native iOS and Android apps will be available soon. All features are accessible through your smartphone.

Troubleshooting

Solutions for common issues

Login Issues

  • Check if Caps Lock is on
  • Verify your email address is correct
  • Use the password reset option
  • Clear browser cookies and cache
  • Try a different browser

Appointment Issues

  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify appointment slot availability
  • Ensure system time is correct
  • Contact support if issue persists

File Upload Issues

  • Check file size (maximum 5MB)
  • Use supported formats (PDF, JPG, PNG)
  • Ensure stable internet connection
  • Try reloading the page
  • Contact support for assistance

Payment Issues

  • Verify all card details are correct
  • Check if card is active and not expired
  • Try a different payment method
  • Contact your bank for assistance
  • Reach out to our support team

Message Issues

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify message content and format
  • Refresh the browser page
  • Try sending again after a moment
  • Contact support if still failing

Other Issues

  • Check system status page
  • Clear cache and cookies
  • Update to latest browser version
  • Disable browser extensions
  • Contact our support team
